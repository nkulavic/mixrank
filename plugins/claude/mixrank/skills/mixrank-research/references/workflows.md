# Reusable research modes

## Vertical landscape

Translate the vertical into candidate taxonomy IDs, free-text terms, geography and exclusions. Compare a small sample from each plausible interpretation; for example, “healthtech” may include care providers, software vendors and device manufacturers. Keep those segments distinct if their buyer needs differ. Use industries to clarify taxonomy; company search to discover; match to resolve known inputs. Report estimated population with query and count limitations, segment boundaries and representative accounts. Do not equate search recall with a complete market census.

## Targeted accounts

Combine selected profile capabilities and buyer hypotheses with user criteria. A useful priority model separates required eligibility, observed fit signals, evidence confidence and optional timing signals. Explain criteria before scoring and preserve unscored unknowns. Deduplicate subsidiaries only when the user wants parent-level accounts; otherwise retain them with parent relationships. Return account ID, canonical domain, fit rationale, contradictory evidence, dates, role hypotheses and next research step.

## Account brief and contact map

Resolve the account, read cached detail, inspect sparse historical observations and relevant posts. Find people whose current nested experience matches both the account and relevant role criteria. A historical employer association is not sufficient. Retain multiple plausible decision makers with their actual roles and evidence. Append business contact types only as requested; do not label returned contacts validated unless validation evidence exists. A brief should tie each inference to observations and distinguish proposed next steps from completed actions.

## Technology targeting

Choose website tags for web technologies or app SDKs for mobile. Join app/developer/company identifiers using documented relationships. Distinguish installed, newly observed, removed and last-seen technologies. A removed SDK is not a current install. Technology use suggests technical fit; it does not imply a contract is expiring or a buyer is dissatisfied.

## List enrichment

Keep the original input row identifier. Match names/domains/URLs first, preserve candidate sets, and annotate unresolved or conflicting rows. Enrich only resolved rows; keep failed rows with error and retry status. Batch email validations through a job, record its ID, and inspect/download when complete. Do not resubmit an uncertain job. Verify output count against accepted/invalid/processed counts and retain unmatched rows. Export in the requested schema with source dates and explicit nulls. Do not overwrite the input file.

## Outreach drafts

When requested, draft messages from verified account-specific observations and the selected product context. Claims about the product must be supported by the profile; claims about the prospect must be supported by research. Avoid sensitive inferences, fabricated familiarity and invented pain. Provide drafts as files or text for review; sending and sequencing remain outside this toolkit.
